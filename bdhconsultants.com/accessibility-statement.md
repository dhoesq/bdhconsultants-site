# Accessibility Statement

**BDH Consultants** · bdhconsultants.com
**Last reviewed:** [EFFECTIVE DATE — set on publish]

BDH Consultants LLC wants bdhconsultants.com to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, voice control, or captions.

## Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as our target for new work. We test with automated tools, keyboard-only navigation, and a screen reader, and we review accessibility whenever we change the Site.

## What we have done

- Pages use a declared language, a logical heading order, and landmarks for navigation, main content, and footer;
- Meaningful images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology;
- Every form field has a visible label, and errors and confirmations are announced;
- All functions work with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator;
- Text meets WCAG color-contrast minimums; and
- Motion respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.

## Known limitations

Some third-party tools we link to (such as scheduling or payment pages) are run by other companies and may not fully meet our standard. If one of them gives you trouble, contact us and we will help you another way.
