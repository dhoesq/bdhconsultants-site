## Cookies, analytics, and tracking

We use strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies to deliver and secure the Site. These are always on.

We also use analytics tools that set cookies or similar identifiers. **They do not load until you choose "Accept analytics" in our privacy choices banner.** You can change your choice at any time with the **Your Privacy Choices** link in the footer. We do not use advertising or remarketing pixels.

**Other parties' tracking across sites.** When analytics are on, the analytics providers listed above may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites. When analytics are off, they do not receive that information from our Site.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for this browser, we keep optional analytics off, and our privacy choices banner confirms the signal was honored. Because there is no common standard for the older "Do Not Track" browser signal, we treat it the same way we treat GPC: optional analytics stay off unless you turn them on.

## Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and we will respond within 45 days.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to:

- **Know and access** the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, the sources, our purposes, and who we disclose it to;
- **Delete** personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions;
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **Opt out of sale or sharing** (we do neither, and we honor Global Privacy Control as described above);
- **Limit the use of sensitive personal information** (we do not collect it); and
- **Not be discriminated against** for using any of these rights.
